Small self-contained things built to answer a question, learn a technique, or tell a joke. One file where I can get away with it, no build step, no dependencies.

## 110yd

A ruler 110 yards long, running down the left edge of the page. It is marked in inches, halves, quarters, and eighths, with a red line and a label at every yard. Scroll to the bottom and it tells you so.

Only the marks near the viewport exist in the DOM. The rest are built as they come into range and thrown away as they leave, which is what keeps a 3960 inch page scrolling smoothly. A readout in the margin shows how many elements are alive at any moment.

The name is the whole reason it exists: 110yd reads as "Lloyd".
